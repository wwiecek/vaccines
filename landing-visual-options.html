Optimal dosing of vaccines

Changing vaccine doses to protect more people

Read the executive summary ↗

For many vaccines, supply is often insufficient to vaccinate everyone who needs them. Optimising doses has saved lives during outbreaks, but both the science and decision-making around optimisation remain poorly understood. I hope these notes are helpful to people interested in working on this problem.

This is a shallow investigation into the optimal use of vaccines, written in early 2024, with some subsequent revisions.

Witold Więcek

Basics of optimal dosing

Biology ↗ Why can a lower dose prove just as effective? Dose response, correlates of protection, and increasing gaps between doses.
