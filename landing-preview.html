Optimal dosing of vaccines

Changing vaccine doses to protect more people

Read the executive summary ↗

For many vaccines, supply is often insufficient to vaccinate everyone who needs them. Adjusting vaccine doses has saved lives during outbreaks, but both the science of dosing and decision-making around optimisation remain poorly understood.

This webpage is supposed to be an accessible introduction to the topic. The material is divided into topic and you don't have to read it in order. Information is compiled from a few white papers and journal publications I wrote on optimal dosing of vaccines. A lot of this material was written in early 2024 and I am working on updating it.

All of the words on this web page were written by me, with AI used for fact checking and presentation.

Witold Więcek
